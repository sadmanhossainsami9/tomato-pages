Tips for an accurate result

Get a clearer answer

One leaf

Photograph a single leaf and fill most of the frame with it.

Good light

Use daylight and keep the photo sharp, not blurry.

Plain background

A simple background works better than a busy one.

Retake if unsure

If the result says it is not sure, retake the photo and scan again.
